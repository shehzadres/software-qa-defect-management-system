# Exploratory Charter ET-01
**Target:** TaskFlow 1.4 RC | **Time box:** 45 minutes

- **Session/access (15m):** login/logout, expired session, back button, direct URLs, cross-user access. Capture role, resource owner, action, outcome and evidence.
- **Task lifecycle (20m):** rapid transitions, repeated submit, empty/long title, assignment changes, punctuation in comments, refresh during edits. Watch for duplicates, stale UI and invalid states.
- **Search/recovery (10m):** empty, whitespace, mixed case, punctuation, unicode and no-result behavior.

Log observations in `test-cases/exploratory-notes.csv`. Promote findings to defects only after reproducible evidence and requirement comparison. Seeded notes are simulated examples, not live discoveries.
